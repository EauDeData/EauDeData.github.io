FACTOR. Default basis is the Earth's revolution, 365.256 days or 3.17 × 10⁻⁸ Hz, raised to 440 Hz by 1.389 × 10¹⁰ (about 33.7 octaves). Choose another basis or pitch and that single factor is recomputed; every ratio between motions stays exact.

PERIODS. Sidereal throughout. The Earth's day is 23 h 56 m and sounds 366.26 times higher than its year. Each pad's LED blinks at its own pitch, octaves down.

LOG. Each ratio to the basis is raised to the power k: f = basis × ratiok. Order and relative spacing survive while the extremes come toward the ear. The far ends still escape it.

UNHEARD. Below 20 Hz or above 20 kHz a channel stays silent: its pad turns hatched, its trace dotted, its spectrum mark hollow.

PLATTER. Planets at their true mean places (J2000 elements); minor bodies and moons approximate. Distances logarithmic. The strobe rim turns with the Earth's year. Fast motions alias like wagon wheels; lower the pace.

KEYS. 1 to 9 fire hot cues, space plays and pauses, + and − zoom, 0 fits, F follows the last body you touched. Drag knobs vertically, double-click to reset. On iPhone the silent switch mutes web audio.
